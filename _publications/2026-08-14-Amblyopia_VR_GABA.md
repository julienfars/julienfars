**Manuscript in preparation (as of August 2026).**

This study investigates whether brief dichoptic virtual reality training can improve binocular vision and reduce GABAergic inhibition, measured with magnetic resonance spectroscopy (MRS), in the visual cortex of adults with amblyopia. It builds on the VR rehabilitation training and MRS analysis pipeline described under [Code]({{ base_path }}/code/) and presented at the [AVA 30th Xmas Meeting]({{ base_path }}/talks/2025-12-15-ava-xmas-meeting-gaba-amblyopia).

This page will be updated with the full citation and links once the manuscript is submitted/published.
