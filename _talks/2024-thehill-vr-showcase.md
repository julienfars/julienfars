Exhibited a virtual reality training programme for amblyopia (lazy eye) treatment, developed at the Wellcome Centre for Integrative Neuroimaging (WIN), University of Oxford, with Dr Betina Ip and Prof. Holly Bridge. The VR headset adapts a standard binocular vision training protocol: the amblyopic eye is presented with the task while the fellow eye receives distracting noise, which is reduced as performance improves.
