Talk given at the 30th Applied Vision Association (AVA) Xmas Meeting, held at the Vision Sciences Building, Aston University, Birmingham, on Monday 15th December 2025.

**Presenter:** Juju Fars, Oxford Centre for Human Brain Activity (OxCIN), Nuffield Department of Clinical Neurosciences (NDCN), University of Oxford

The talk reported on effects of visual training on perception and GABA concentration in the early visual cortex of amblyopes.
